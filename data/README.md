# Data

`datatraining.txt`, `datatest.txt` and `datatest2.txt` are the Occupancy Detection dataset of
Candanedo & Feldheim (2016), redistributed unchanged under its CC BY 4.0 licence.

* Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5X01N
  (mirror: https://github.com/LuisM78/Occupancy-detection-data)
* Columns: date, Temperature (°C), Humidity (% RH), Light (lux), CO2 (ppm), HumidityRatio (kg/kg), Occupancy (0/1)
* Combined after removing duplicate timestamps: 20,560 one-minute records, 2015-02-02 14:19 to 2015-02-18 09:19.

Please cite: Candanedo, L. M., & Feldheim, V. (2016). Accurate occupancy detection of an office room from light,
temperature, humidity and CO2 measurements using statistical learning models. *Energy and Buildings, 112*, 28–39.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2015.11.071
